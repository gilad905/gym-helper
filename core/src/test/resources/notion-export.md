### Full Body Workout

- **1. Crosstrainer**
    
    ![103864.jpg](Gym/103864.jpg)
    
    - **Sets:** 1
    - **Duration:** 10 minutes
    - **Instructions:** 5–10 minute warmup with moderate intensity.
- **2. Beinstreckung (14) — Leg Extension**
    
    ![103863.jpg](Gym/103863.jpg)
    
    - **Sets:** 3
    - **Reps:** 15
    - **Weight:** 15 kg
    - **Rest:** 1 min 30 sec
    - **Knee**: 2
    - **Ankle:** 3
    - **Back**: 5
    - **Instructions:** Pull your toes towards your body, chest comes up. Upper body shouldn't fold.
- **3. Rudern sitzend (03) — Seated Row**
    
    ![103862.jpg](Gym/103862.jpg)
    
    - **Sets:** 3
    - **Reps:** 12 - 15
    - **Weight:** 15 kg
    - **Rest:** 1 min 30 sec
    - **Chest**: 5
    - **Seat:** 7
    - **Instructions:** Chest up, elbows don't twist. shoulders down. Crunch the angel muscles. Pull up to where hands are in line with breast
- **4. Latzug (05) — Lat Pulldown**
    
    ![103859.jpg](Gym/103859.jpg)
    
    - **Sets:** 3
    - **Reps:** 12 - 15
    - **Weight:** 25 kg
    - **Rest:** 1 min 30 sec
    - **Seat height:** 5
    - **Instructions:** Shoulders come down first, elbows turned inside, arms follow.
- **5. Brustpresse (01) — Chest Press**
    
    ![103860.jpg](Gym/103860.jpg)
    
    - **Sets:** 3
    - **Reps:** 12
    - **Weight:** 10 kg - 15
    - **Rest:** 1 min 30 sec
    - **Seat height:** 7
    - **Instructions:** Chest comes up and stays there, make sure your neck is not under tension.
- **6. Schulterpresse (06) — Shoulder Press**
    
    ![103861.jpg](Gym/103861.jpg)
    
    - **Sets:** 3
    - **Reps:** 12
    - **Weight:** 10 kg
    - **Rest:** 1 min 30 sec
    - **Seat:** 7
    - Notes: shoulders down
- **7. Bauchmaschine II (18) — Abdominal Machine II**
    
    ![103857.jpg](Gym/103857.jpg)
    
    - **Sets:** 3
    - **Reps:** 12 - 15
    - **Weight:** 10 kg
    - **Rest:** 1 min 30 sec
    - Notes: don’t go all the way back, chin touches the chest, focus on belly
- **8. Sitzfahrrad — Stationary Bike**
    
    ![103858.jpg](Gym/103858.jpg)
    
    - **Sets:** 1
    - **Duration:** 10 minutes
    - **Instructions:** 10 minutes at moderate pace.
    - **Rpm:** 70/75
    - **Resistance:** 3
